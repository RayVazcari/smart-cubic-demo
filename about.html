Our Story

Built by Someone Who's Run the Job Site, Not Just the Office

Smart Cubic was founded on a simple standard: treat every property like it's your own family's home.

From Civil Engineering to Water Mitigation

Our founder has worked as a civil engineer and builder since 1992, with hands-on experience across construction, remodeling, and structural repair. A hands-on introduction to the mitigation trade — and a certification earned in Houston in 2024 — turned that construction background toward emergency water response.

That experience showed a gap: San Antonio homeowners deserve the same technical rigor as major commercial jobs, delivered with the personal attention only a local, family-operated crew can give. Smart Cubic exists to close that gap, starting here at home.

Today, our bilingual team brings that same engineering-first approach to every job — whether it's a single water leak or a full restoration — with direct communication in the language each family is most comfortable in.
